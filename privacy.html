1. Information we keep

  • Account: your email address, a protected (hashed) password handled by Firebase Authentication, your name, username, bio and profile photo link.
  • What you create: posts and their photo links, comments, likes, saved posts, stories, story views and reactions, messages in your chats, and who you follow or who follows you.
  • Activity and settings: whether you are online and when you were last active (only if "Show my active status" is on), your blocked accounts, notifications, and your settings.
  • Reports and feedback: posts you report and problems you send us, with the details you choose to add.
  • Technical data: basic data that our service providers receive automatically when the app runs, such as your IP address and device/browser type.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to run FreeZone BD: to log you in, show your profile and posts to the right people, deliver messages and notifications, keep the app safe, handle reports, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who can see what

  • Your name, username, photo, bio and posts are visible to other signed-in users of FreeZone BD.
  • Your stories are visible to the people who follow you.
  • Your chats are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • Your online status is visible only to people you share it with, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Your email address is never shown on your profile.

Messages are protected by access rules but are not end-to-end encrypted. Please do not send passwords, bank details or other highly sensitive information in chat.

4. Services we use

FreeZone BD relies on trusted providers to work:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) stores accounts and app data.
  • imgbb hosts the photos you upload. We save only the link to your photo in our database.
  • Google Fonts and Tailwind CDN load the app's fonts and styles.

These providers have their own privacy policies and process data on our behalf.

5. Your choices and control

  • Edit your name (once every 60 days), photo and bio in Settings > Edit profile.
  • Hide your active status in Settings > Show my active status.
  • Block accounts, report posts, and delete your own posts, comments and chats at any time.
  • Delete your whole account in Settings > Delete account.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, stories, follows, chats, saved posts, notifications and settings are deleted from our database. Comments you wrote on other people's posts may remain until those posts are removed. Photos are stored by our image host; we remove every link to them from FreeZone BD, but the image file itself may stay at the host for some time.

Reports and feedback may be kept for a limited time so that we can keep the community safe.

7. Security

Access to data is limited by database rules, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and log out on shared devices.

8. Children

FreeZone BD is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please tell us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Reach us through the Report a problem option in Settings.

This page is a general policy written for FreeZone BD. If you run the app commercially, ask a lawyer in your country to review it.
