Community Guidelines

Last updated:

FreeZone BD is a place to connect, share and chat with friends. These guidelines keep it friendly and safe for everyone. If something here is broken, you can report it or block the account.

1. Be kind and respectful

Treat others the way you want to be treated. Disagreeing is fine; insulting, bullying or humiliating people is not.

  • No harassment, threats or repeated unwanted messages
  • No hate speech or attacks on people for their religion, race, gender, disability, nationality or background

2. Keep it safe

  • No violence, or content that encourages hurting yourself or others
  • No sexual or nude content
  • Never post anything that sexualises or endangers children. This leads to immediate removal and may be reported to the authorities

3. Be honest

  • No fake accounts and no pretending to be another person, a company or an official
  • No scams, fraud or tricks to get money or passwords
  • No false information that can cause harm, for example fake news during emergencies

4. Respect privacy

  • Do not share other people's private information (phone numbers, addresses, private photos or chats) without their permission
  • Do not post photos of others in private moments without consent

5. No spam

  • No repeated posts, mass messages or fake likes and follows
  • No unwanted advertising or chain messages

6. Follow the law

Do not use FreeZone BD for anything illegal, including selling illegal items or sharing pirated or stolen material.

7. Your tools

  • Report a post from its ⋮ menu.
  • Block an account from its profile or from Settings > Blocked accounts. They are not told, and you will no longer see each other.
  • Delete your own posts, comments, messages and chats whenever you like.

8. What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the content, limit some features, or suspend or delete the account. Serious or repeated breaking of these guidelines can lead to a permanent ban.

9. Contact

Need help or want to appeal a decision? Reach us through the Report a problem option in Settings.

This page is a general policy written for FreeZone BD. If you run the app commercially, ask a lawyer in your country to review it.
